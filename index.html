🎯 2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to power the SpeakMate experience:

  • Personalised Greeting: The name you enter during onboarding is used to greet you by name on the Home screen and in relevant places throughout the app.
  • Real-Time AI Coaching: Your conversation messages are sent to OpenAI (gpt-4o-mini) to generate grammar corrections, vocabulary suggestions, fluency tips, pronunciation notes, and scores in real time.
  • Lessons & Learning Path: Structured lesson content grouped by level and goal is fetched from Cloud Firestore. Your position in the path — current unit/lesson, completed lessons, streak, and XP — is read from and written to Firestore so your progress unlocks the right content and is not lost if you reinstall on the same account session.
  • Role-Play Missions: Your messages are sent to OpenAI with a custom role-play system prompt so the AI stays in character as the specified scenario partner.
  • Progress Tracking: Scores and session records stored on-device power your Progress dashboard, skill bars, and weekly streak calendar; summary totals (streak, XP, sessions, minutes) are mirrored to Firestore.
  • AI Voice Responses: The AI coach's text reply is sent to Google Cloud TTS to synthesise natural-sounding speech, cached locally for instant replay.
  • Banner & Interstitial Ads (AdMob): Google AdMob banner ads and interstitial ads are displayed to support the free experience. Ad content is determined by Google based on contextual signals and, where personalisation is enabled, your Advertising ID (GAID).
  • Rewarded Ads (AdMob): In Practice mode, roughly every 2 minutes of active back-and-forth conversation, the mic locks after the AI finishes speaking and switches to "Watch Ad to Continue" — never mid-recording, only between turns. In Lessons, a similar gate appears before the first Speaking activity of a lesson, with an explicit "Not Now" option. In both cases, watching (or briefly viewing and closing) the ad unlocks the mic again; this exists because continuing to converse with the AI coach costs us real, per-use fees (OpenAI and Google Cloud TTS) on your behalf. If a rewarded ad fails to load or show in Practice mode, the mic does not unlock for free — it stays gated while the app quietly retries loading another ad in the background, and your only way to end the wait is to tap "End Session." If a mission is in progress when the gate appears, its countdown timer is paused for the duration of the gate so you don't lose mission time waiting on an ad. No in-app currency or bonus XP is granted for watching.
  • Forced / Mandatory App Updates: Firebase Remote Config checks whether your app version meets the minimum required version. If not, you'll see a mandatory update prompt via the Google Play In-App Updates API or the Play Store.
  • Daily & Mission Reminders: Android WorkManager schedules local device notifications — no data leaves your device for this feature.
  • Crash & Stability Improvements: Anonymous crash reports help us identify and fix technical issues.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous event counts help us understand which features are most used so we can improve the app.

We do not use your data for advertising profiling, data brokering, or any purpose beyond what is listed above.

🔐 3. App Permissions

SpeakMate requests the following Android permissions. Each is strictly necessary. Adding Lessons and Firestore sync did not require any new permission beyond the existing INTERNET permission:

  • 🎤 RECORD_AUDIO — Required for voice-to-text input. Audio is processed on-device — raw audio is never uploaded to our servers.
  • 🔍 Speech Recognition Query — SpeakMate declares a <queries> intent for android.speech.RecognitionService so Android 11+ can discover the Google Speech Recognition service. No data is collected by this declaration.
  • 🌐 INTERNET — Required to communicate with OpenAI, Google Cloud TTS, Google AdMob, Firebase Remote Config, Cloud Firestore, and other Firebase services.
  • 📡 ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Allows the app to check connectivity before API calls and before fetching remote config or lesson content.
  • 🔔 POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Required on Android 13+ to send daily practice and mission reminders. You explicitly opt in via the Settings screen.
  • 📳 VIBRATE — Used for haptic feedback when the microphone starts recording.
  • 🔄 RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — Required to reschedule reminder notifications after device restarts. The app also listens for QUICKBOOT_POWERON for the same purpose.
  • 📢 AD_ID — Automatically added by the Google AdMob SDK (play-services-ads) via manifest merging. Used to access your device's Advertising ID (GAID) to serve relevant personalised or contextual ads. You can opt out via Google Settings → Ads.

📚 5. Lessons, Firestore & Your Progress

Version 1.2.2 introduces structured Lessons and moves part of your profile and progress from purely on-device storage into Google Cloud Firestore. This section explains exactly what changed.

How your anonymous account is created: The first time you complete onboarding, SpeakMate silently signs you in to Firebase Authentication using anonymous sign-in — no email, phone number, or password is ever asked for or collected. This creates a random ID (e.g. a1B2c3D4...) that lives in the app's private storage on your device.

What's saved under your anonymous account (users/{"{"}your-anonymous-id{"}"}) in Firestore:

  • The first name you entered during onboarding.
  • Your selected learning level and goal (e.g. "Beginner" / "Travel English").
  • Your current path, unit, and lesson position, and which units/lessons are completed or unlocked.
  • Your streak count, total XP, total completed sessions, and total minutes practiced.
  • The date you completed onboarding.

What is not sent to Firestore: your raw voice recordings, your full chat transcripts, per-message AI feedback/scores, or your device's advertising identifier. Those remain on your device only, as described in Section 1.

Lesson content itself (the units, prompts, and exercises you complete) lives in a separate, shared part of Firestore that is the same for every user — it is general educational content, not your personal data, and is simply downloaded to your device so lessons load quickly.

Important — this is a change from earlier versions: Prior to 1.2.2, SpeakMate stated that "there is no server-side database containing your profile." That is no longer accurate. Starting in 1.2.2, the fields listed above are stored on our servers (via Google Cloud Firestore) so that your name, level, goal, and progress can be reliably kept in sync. See Section 6 for retention and Section 9 for how to delete this data.

💾 6. Data Storage & Retention

On-Device Storage: Your full conversation history, session records, per-message scores, and mission completion status are stored in an encrypted SQLite database (Room). Your app settings, onboarding draft, and cached lesson position are stored via Android SharedPreferences, and your avatar selection via Android DataStore. None of this local-only data is synced to Firestore.

Cloud Firestore Storage (New): Your name, selected level/goal, current lesson/unit position, streak, XP, session, and minute totals are stored on Google Cloud Firestore servers, keyed to your random anonymous account ID (see Section 5). This lets your Lessons progress be restored reliably even if local app data is cleared while your anonymous session is still active.

Audio Cache: TTS-generated audio files are cached on your device's internal storage. Clearable at any time via Settings → Clear Voice Cache or via Android's app storage management.

Retention Period — On-Device Data: Retained until you use "Reset Progress" in Settings, uninstall the app, or clear the app's data via Android's App Info settings.

Retention Period — Firestore Data: Retained on our servers for as long as your anonymous account is active, and afterward for backup/operational purposes, or until you request deletion (Section 9). Because sign-in is anonymous, note the following:

  • Using Reset Progress in Settings resets your streak, XP, sessions, and lesson/unit progress both locally and in Firestore, but does not delete your stored name or your Firestore record entirely.
  • Uninstalling the app removes your local anonymous session. A fresh install creates a brand-new anonymous ID and a new, empty profile — your previous Firestore record is not automatically deleted and may persist on our servers unless you request deletion beforehand (Section 9) or it is removed under our retention schedule.

OpenAI API (Transient): Messages sent to OpenAI are subject to OpenAI's own data retention policies. We do not store copies on our servers.

Firebase Remote Config (Transient): No personal data is retained — only anonymous fetch counts are logged by Firebase.

AdMob Data: Data collected by Google AdMob is retained and managed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Crash/Analytics Data: Anonymous crash and analytics data is retained by Google Firebase for up to 90 days.

🛡️ 7. Security

  • Encrypted Local Storage: All on-device data is stored in an encrypted Room database protected by Android's built-in encryption.
  • Firestore Access Control: Cloud Firestore security rules restrict every user document so that only the matching authenticated anonymous account can read or write it — your data is never readable by another user's session.
  • HTTPS Only: All network communications, including Firestore, use TLS/HTTPS. Cleartext traffic is explicitly disabled in the app manifest.
  • API Key Security: Third-party API keys are injected at build time via BuildConfig and obfuscated via ProGuard/R8 in release builds.
  • Signed APK: The app is signed with v1, v2, and v3 APK signing schemes to ensure integrity.
  • Forced Updates: Firebase Remote Config enforces a minimum app version so users always run a version with the latest security patches.
  • Minimal Data Collection: We follow the principle of data minimisation — collecting only what is strictly necessary, and keeping the most sensitive data (raw audio, full transcripts) on-device only.
  • Ad Placement Policy: AdMob banner and interstitial ads comply with Google Play Ad Policy. In Lessons, the rewarded ad gate offers a visible "Not Now" option that exits the lesson without watching. In Practice mode, the gate is time-based (roughly every 2 minutes of live conversation) and only ever appears between AI turns, never interrupting your own recording; the mic stays gated until an ad is shown, and "End Session" remains available as an exit at any time.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to keep your device updated and secured with a screen lock.

⚖️ 9. Your Rights

  • Access: All your data is visible directly inside the app in the Home, Sessions, Progress, and Lessons screens.
  • Local Deletion: Use Settings → Reset Progress to delete locally stored sessions, XP, streaks, and lesson progress (this also clears the matching Firestore fields). Uninstalling the app removes all remaining local data.
  • Full Cloud Deletion: Because your name and progress summary now live in Cloud Firestore, deleting them completely requires contacting us at the email in Section 13 — since your account is anonymous, please reach out from within the app (Settings → Contact/Support, if available) or shortly after using the app so we can help locate the correct record, or simply request "delete all my SpeakMate Firestore data" and we will assist.
  • Opt-Out of Ad Personalisation: Go to Google Settings → Ads on your device to reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads. Non-personalised contextual ads will still be shown.
  • Opt-Out of Analytics: You can disable Firebase Analytics via Google's device-level settings.
  • Notification Control: Disable reminders at any time via Settings → Notifications or your device's notification settings.
  • Microphone Access: Revoke microphone permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → SpeakMate → Permissions. The app will still function in text-only mode.
  • GDPR / CCPA: If you are in the European Economic Area or California, you have additional rights including data portability, correction, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

🔄 11. Forced / Mandatory App Updates

SpeakMate uses Firebase Remote Config combined with the Google Play In-App Updates API to deliver a two-layer forced update mechanism, protecting users from running outdated or insecure app versions.

  • Step 1 — Version check via Firebase Remote Config: Each time you open SpeakMate, the app silently fetches a min_version_code flag from Firebase Remote Config, compared against your currently installed app version entirely on-device.
  • Step 2 — Google Play In-App Updates (primary): If your version is below the minimum, SpeakMate attempts an IMMEDIATE update flow using the official Google Play In-App Updates API, shown directly inside the app.
  • Step 3 — Fallback dialog: If the Play Core update is unavailable, a non-dismissible dialog directs you to the Google Play Store. The app closes after you tap "Update Now."
  • You cannot use the app until the update is installed, ensuring all users benefit from critical bug fixes and security patches.
Privacy note: The forced update check does not transmit any personal data. Firebase Remote Config only delivers a version number flag to your device — your installed version is compared locally, not sent to our servers.

We aim to minimise the frequency of forced updates and only trigger them when strictly necessary for security, stability, or compatibility reasons.
