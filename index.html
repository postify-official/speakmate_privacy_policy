2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to power the SpeakMate coaching experience:

  • Real-Time AI Coaching: Your conversation messages are sent to our AI provider to generate grammar corrections, vocabulary suggestions, and pronunciation tips in real time.
  • Progress Tracking: Scores and session records stored on-device power your personal Progress dashboard, skill charts, and streak calendar.
  • AI Voice Responses: Your text messages are sent to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (TTS) to synthesise natural-sounding AI voice replies in the accent you select.
  • Notifications & Reminders: If you enable daily reminders, we schedule local device notifications via WorkManager — no data leaves your device for this feature.
  • Crash & Stability Improvements: Anonymous crash reports are used solely to identify and fix technical issues.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous event counts help us understand which features are most used so we can improve the app.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond what is listed above.

3. App Permissions

SpeakMate requests the following Android permissions. Each is strictly necessary:

  • 🎤
    RECORD_AUDIO Required for voice-to-text input. You tap the microphone button to start recording. Audio is processed on-device by Android's Speech Recognizer — raw audio is never uploaded to our servers.
  • 🌐
    INTERNET Required to communicate with the AI coaching API, TTS voice APIs, and Firebase services for crash reporting and analytics.
  • 📡
    ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Allows the app to check if your device is connected before making API calls, so we can display a helpful "No internet connection" error rather than a generic failure.
  • 🔔
    POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13+ to send daily practice reminders. You explicitly opt in via the Settings screen. Notifications can be disabled at any time.
  • 📳
    VIBRATE Used for subtle haptic feedback when the microphone starts recording, providing clear tactile confirmation.
  • 🔄
    RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Required to reschedule your daily reminder notification after your device restarts. Without this, notifications would disappear after a reboot.

5. Data Storage & Retention

On-Device Storage: Your conversation history, session records, progress scores, XP, achievements, and user preferences are stored in an encrypted SQLite database (Room) and Android DataStore on your device. This data remains on your device and is not synced to any cloud service operated by us.

Retention Period: On-device data is retained until you:

  • Use the "Reset Progress" option in Settings (clears all XP, sessions, streaks, and progress).
  • Uninstall the SpeakMate app from your device.
  • Clear the app's data via Android's App Info settings.

AI API (Transient): Conversation messages sent to the OpenAI API are used to generate responses and are subject to OpenAI's data retention policies. We do not store copies of these transmissions on our own servers.

Firebase Data: Anonymous crash and analytics data is retained by Google Firebase for up to 90 days in accordance with Firebase's default retention settings.

No Server-Side User Database: SpeakMate does not operate a user account system. There is no server-side database containing your profile, conversation history, or personal information maintained by us.

6. Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted Local Storage: All on-device data is stored in an encrypted Room database protected by Android's built-in encryption.
  • HTTPS Only: All network communications use TLS/HTTPS encryption. Cleartext traffic is explicitly disabled (android:usesCleartextTraffic="false") in our app manifest.
  • API Key Security: Third-party API keys are injected at build time and obfuscated via ProGuard/R8 code shrinking in release builds. Keys are never hard-coded in accessible source.
  • Signed APK: The app is signed with v1, v2, and v3 APK signing schemes to ensure integrity and prevent tampering.
  • Minimal Data Collection: We follow the principle of data minimisation — collecting only what is strictly necessary for the app to function.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to keep your device updated and secured with a screen lock.

8. Your Rights

You have meaningful control over your data within SpeakMate:

  • Access: All your data (sessions, scores, history) is visible directly inside the app in the Sessions and Progress screens.
  • Deletion: Use Settings → Reset Progress to delete all locally stored data at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all remaining local data.
  • Correction: Session transcripts and feedback are read-only records of AI interactions and cannot be individually edited, but you can delete all sessions via the Reset function.
  • Opt-Out of Analytics: You can disable Firebase Analytics data collection by toggling off analytics in your device's Google settings, or by disabling personalised ads in Google's ad settings.
  • Notification Control: Disable practice reminders at any time via Settings → Notifications or your device's notification settings.
  • Microphone Access: You can revoke microphone permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → SpeakMate → Permissions. The app will still function in text-only mode.
GDPR / CCPA: If you are located in the European Economic Area or California, you have additional rights including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise these rights.
